Frequently Asked Questions
Do we have to engage all four areas?
No. Each engagement stands on its own. Most clients start with one or two, where the constraint is, and connect the rest later if it earns its place.
How do you decide what to work on first?
We diagnose before we deliver. We find the one thing setting the ceiling on the next stage, then scope the work around lifting it, rather than selling a fixed package.
Can you work alongside our in-house team?
Yes. We build with your team, not around it. The work is designed to hand over cleanly and to keep running once it does.
What kind of companies do you work best with?
Companies with a proven offer and real momentum that want the next stage built to a higher standard. If the product still needs to find its market, we are not the right partner yet.
Finite or Infinite, which is right for us?
Finite if you have one defined brief and need decisive progress. Infinite if you are building over time and want strategy, execution and optimisation that adapts as the priorities change. We will tell you honestly which fits.
